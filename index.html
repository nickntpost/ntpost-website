NT Post Ltd
VFX, online, titles and conform for television & film

NT Post is a Flame VFX and online company, founded in 2026 by Nick Timms, a Flame artist and senior online editor with twenty years in post production.

Nick has worked on BAFTA- and Emmy-winning shows for broadcasters and streaming platforms, at Encore, Technicolor, Picture Shop and The Farm Soho. Along the way he has collaborated closely with renowned writers, directors, producers, VFX supervisors and post producers, shaping the visual effects, titles and credits on their shows.
